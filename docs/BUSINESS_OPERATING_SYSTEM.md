# Business Operating System

Opportunity OS treats sales as a measurable service system, not a message-volume contest.

## Additional operating library

- **Founding Sales — Pete Kazanjy:** personally learn the full motion before scaling; measure prospecting, qualification, proposal and closing separately.
- **The Mom Test — Rob Fitzpatrick:** validate pain through recent behavior, consequences and commitment—not compliments or hypothetical interest.
- **Obviously Awesome — April Dunford:** position the service against the customer's real alternative for the segment that values the differentiated capability.
- **Lean Analytics — Alistair Croll and Benjamin Yoskovitz:** select a phase-appropriate primary metric. Here it is qualified expected revenue per hour, with relationship and safety guardrails.
- **The Trusted Advisor — Maister, Green and Galford:** grow professional relationships through credibility, reliability, context and client-first intent.
- **Customer Success — Mehta, Steinman and Murphy:** track desired client outcome, next value milestone, health and retention risk.
- **Predictable Revenue — Aaron Ross and Marylou Tyler:** keep acquisition stages analytically separate. Opportunity OS does not copy volume-first tactics or treat them as universally applicable.

The product stores original operational paraphrases, not book excerpts.

## Forecast model

The model is assumption-based and never presented as guaranteed income:

`target accounts × qualification rate × proposal rate × win rate × engagement value`

Recognized revenue is also capped by delivery capacity. New engagements are modeled as starting halfway through each horizon. Required horizons are 5, 15, 30, 90, 180, 365 and 1,095 days. Every saved forecast must retain its input assumptions and model version.

## Professional-sales firewall

Outreach is blocked when the recipient is unverified, relevance/evidence is missing, the contact opted out, claims are unverified, follow-up frequency is unsafe, or human approval is absent. No autonomous sending is permitted.

## Operating cadence

Start with verified accounts and real discovery evidence. Position only after recurring problems appear. Scale only a motion with measured stage conversion and safe delivery capacity. An active client must always have a desired outcome, next value milestone, health checkpoint and contact date.
